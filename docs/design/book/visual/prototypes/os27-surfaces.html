Liquid Glass rules for VoidX

The person now chooses how clear or tinted the glass is. We cannot test one look; we test the range.

  • Glass is for the system layer: tab bar, sidebar, toolbar, sheets, menus. Position cards, rows and the range bar stay on opaque colour blocks, so nothing we draw changes with the slider.
  • At most one custom glass element per screen (the refresh or range-status pill), grouped in a GlassEffectContainer, using the regular variant under text.
  • Test the extremes: ultra-clear and fully tinted, light and dark, with Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast on. A toolbar over a coloured block must keep 4.5 to 1 for its labels. This is a manual check; the token test does not cover glass.
  • Colour on glass is for the primary action and status only. No coloured controls on toolbars, and no label colour close to the block behind it.
  • Use the system controls and let them adapt. Where a custom control looks different from a system one at the extremes, replace it.
  • Widgets and Lock Screen controls follow the same slider. They are drawn by the system around our content; we only supply content and an accent group.

Accessibility and tests for these surfaces

Extends docs/decisions/0022-accessibility.md.

  • Widgets are read as one element with a label that says the whole thing ("ETH / BTC.b is out of range, 1,841 dollars"). Rows that are links have their own names.
  • Status never relies on colour. Word plus shape survive accented and vibrant rendering. Check each mode by looking at it in greyscale.
  • Contrast under the system tint. We supply an accent group and an opaque container background; the system does the tinting. Check at the ultra-clear and tinted ends of the glass slider.
  • Text size. Widgets scale with Dynamic Type where the family allows it; truncate nothing that carries the status.
  • No flashing, no motion that matters. Timelines refresh values, they do not animate.
  • Notifications read the pair, the state and the numbers; actions have names; the Mute action never hides a position without saying so.
  • Siri and Shortcuts answers are text first, so VoiceOver and captions get them as well.
  • Tests: snapshot every widget family in full colour, accented and vibrant, light and dark, at default and accessibility text sizes; UI Automator and Glance tests on Android; manual pass with the glass slider at both ends.

Decisions and open points

Recommendations first.

  1. Build with the current Xcode, minimum OS 26, adopt 27 behind availability checks. Confirm when the 27 SDK becomes mandatory for uploads.
  2. MVP surfaces: small and medium Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen circular, rectangular and inline, notifications. Controls, Live Activity and the Mac menu bar item come later.
  3. No custom glass beyond one element per screen, tested at both ends of the slider.
  4. A position and wallet intent set (open, which are out of range) from the start, so Siri, Spotlight and Shortcuts work the same way.
  5. Open: the widget data limit and refresh budget, whether the widget may show fees, and whether range exit becomes Time Sensitive.
