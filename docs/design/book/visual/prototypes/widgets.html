VoidX Widgets

Home Screen: two widgets, both the Ghost card: Small (status, total, range) and Medium (status, total, range and owed fees for both tokens). Plus the Lock Screen widgets. The look carries over closely. What changes is motion and the system's own tinting. Wireframes with real proportions; point sizes vary by device.

Verdict: feasible, with three honest differences

A widget is SwiftUI, so the colour block, the overlapping coins with their ring, the tilted status chip, the big tabular figure and the range bar with its dot and diamond are all drawable. They look like the card in the design.

  • 2. The system tints. In tinted and clear Home Screen styles the system replaces our background and colours. We design that case on purpose (below).
  • 3. Freshness is limited. Reloads run on a daily budget, roughly every 15 to 60 minutes. The widget says how old its numbers are.

Home Screen: Small and Medium

Home Screen on iPhone, iPad and the Mac desktop, Home Screen on Android. Each shows one position and opens it on tap.

Medium on the four colour blocks
  • Small: pair, status chip, total, range bar. Nothing else, so it reads at a glance.
  • Medium: pair and status on one line, the total with owed fees of token 0 and token 1 as two pills beside it, and a short range bar with its two prices underneath. No fee tier or network line.
  • Which position: chosen in the widget settings, or "the worst": out of range first, then the largest.
  • Fees are server-formatted strings in token units, as in the app. The widget adds no USD for fees unless the app already shows it.
  • Whole widget is one tap target to the position detail. No buttons inside.

Three accessory shapes, drawn in one vibrant tint. No full colour here, so the status is a word and a shape, and the range bar is a thin line with a dot.

  • Rectangular: the worst position with its status word, a thin range line, and a second position.
  • Inline: text only: "1 out of range".

Rendering modes on the Home Screen

Full colour is what we draw. The Tinted and Clear styles let the system apply its own look, and the system can remove our backgrounds. Medium in the three modes:

  • Full colour: the four colour blocks.
  • Tinted: one tint, our fills become a darker version of it. Elements we group as accented take the tint; the rest stay neutral.
  • Clear: glass with white content; our background group is removed.
  • In both, colour is gone, so the diamond and the words carry "out of range", and contrast is checked at both ends of the glass slider.

States

Every state we show in the app has a widget version. Numbers are server-formatted strings from the app's snapshot. The widget never computes.

Android widgets

The same design in a simpler build: Glance widgets are a restricted set of components. The range bar is a small bitmap drawn by the app, and the font is the system font.

  • Sizes: 2 by 2 (small) and 4 by 2 (medium), with responsive layouts so each size chooses the best fit.
  • Taps: the whole widget opens its position through one click action.
  • Update: the app or a scheduled job refreshes the snapshot, then updates the widget. No scrolling lists in the MVP.
  • Colour: our palette, not the wallpaper's dynamic colour, as in the app.

Data, refresh and configuration

Follows ADR 0003 (the server computes) and ADR 0019 (the widget reads the app's snapshot and never refreshes tokens).

  • Snapshot. The app writes the last response, with display strings, into the shared container before it asks the widget to reload. The widget formats nothing.
  • Freshness. The widget shows "Updated 9:41" once the data is older than 30 minutes, and the stale state after two hours. Foreground refreshes, background refreshes and an app intent button all reload it without using the budget.
  • Estimates. USD is an estimate, shown with a leading "~" when a token is unpriced, and the card says "partial value".
  • Configuration. Position (the worst, or a chosen one) and block colour (automatic, or 1 to 4). Positions are an entity the system can search, the same one Siri and Spotlight use.
  • Deep link. To the position (route in the OS 27 and surfaces page); if it no longer exists, to Portfolio.
  • Staleness is data, not decoration. A widget that cannot refresh keeps its last value and says so. It never goes blank.

Accessibility, tests and decisions

Extends docs/decisions/0022-accessibility.md.

  • One spoken sentence per card: "ETH / USDC, in range, 23,587 dollars, price inside 3,180 to 3,620, owed fees 0.0123 ETH and 31.40 USDC". The status is never only a colour.
  • Dynamic Type: text grows with the system size; the status and value never truncate. At the largest sizes the pair and chip shrink first; the total, the range and the fees never do.
  • Contrast: every fill keeps 4.5 to 1 for its ink; tinted and clear are checked at both ends of the glass slider.
  • Tests: snapshot each family, in full colour, tinted and clear, light and dark, default and accessibility text sizes; Glance tests on Android; a manual pass with the slider at both ends and Always On.
  • Decision 1 (made): Home Screen has only Small and Medium (one position, with total and owed fees for token 0 and token 1); the Lock Screen keeps its three shapes. Large, Extra large, two-card Medium, controls and Live Activities are out of scope for now.
  • Decision 2: bundle the display font in the iOS widget extension if the licence allows; Android keeps the system font.
  • Decision 3: no Live Activity and no push-updated widgets in the MVP (ADR 0014). The data section above covers freshness without them.
